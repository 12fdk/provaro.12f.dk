How it works

Built for gloves, bad light and one free hand

Three moves, in the order you already work in. Big targets, nothing to save and reopen.

Show these screens on
  1. 1
    Step one

    Shoot the whole job without leaving the camera

    Stay in the viewfinder and work through a room in one go, or pull in as many shots as you like from the camera roll. They land straight in the job, grouped into sections, each with a caption in your own words.

  2. 2
    Step two

    Draw on what the photo doesn’t say by itself

    Arrows, circles, dots, freehand and text callouts, in six colours at a size you set — one tap from the photo you just took. Nothing is burned in: your original stays exactly as the camera saved it, and you can come back days later and add more.

  3. 3
    Step three

    Hand it over before you pack up

    Pick one photo per page, two-up for before and after, or a grid. See the finished PDF and its file size before it goes, then send it the way the customer already talks to you — WhatsApp, iMessage, email, AirDrop or print.

Questions

The things people ask first

Does it really work with no signal?

Yes. Capture, mark-up and the PDF all happen on the phone in your hand. Nothing is uploaded and no step waits on a connection. Send the report when you’re back in range, or over the customer’s wifi at the door.

Is the free version a trial?

No. Free is the whole job — photograph, mark up, export, share — and it doesn’t expire. Pro is a single 19.99 € payment that puts your branding on the report and turns on iCloud sync. There’s no renewal and nothing to cancel.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up, no email to hand over and no password. Open the app and start a report.

Which trades is it for?

Anyone who has to show what they did: plumbers, electricians, roofers, builders, decorators, plasterers, damp and mould specialists, groundworkers, and letting agents doing property inspections.

Where do my photos go?

Onto your device, and nowhere else — unless you send the report yourself. With Pro they also sync through your own iCloud account, not a server of ours. There’s no advertising identifier and no ad network in the app.

Does one purchase cover iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Start the report on the phone at the job and finish it on the iPad in the van. With Pro the two stay in step through your own iCloud.

What languages is it in?

English and Danish.
